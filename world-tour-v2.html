House rules

Cooldown: when a cuisine is cooked, it sits out for the next four cooked Mondays. This stops one favourite cuisine taking over the world tour.

Chaos Mode: every eligible cuisine has an equal chance first, then one of its dishes is chosen at random.

New Stamp: cuisines with untried dishes enter the country draw equally, then an untried dish is chosen. If everything eligible has been tried, it falls back to the full pool.

Family Favourites: country odds stay equal. Once the country is chosen, higher repeat ratings and ❤️ favourites add extra lottery tickets to that country’s dishes.

Favourites: tap the heart beside any dish. In Family Favourites mode, hearts also get a small boost.
